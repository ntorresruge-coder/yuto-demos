      Meet Sextant

      A phone keeps a diary. Sextant reads it.

      Every time a phone connects to something (earbuds, a car, a Wi-Fi network) it writes a line in its records. The lab copies those records out, and Sextant, our analysis tool, sorts them into tables, like a spreadsheet.

      Two clocks

      Phones don't store dates. They count seconds from one fixed moment: midnight on 1 January 2001, in world time (UTC).

      1 Jan 2001
      00:00 UTC      + 794,289,494 s
      World time (UTC)03:58Wed 4 March
      −5 h
      Port Calder22:58Tue 3 March

      Same moment, two clocks. Port Calder is five hours behind world time in winter, so every phone time needs that fix.

      Your finding, worded for court

      The blue iPhone, exhibit EXH-02, was connected to the car stereo CAR AUDIO 7731 from 22:58 to 23:41 on 3 March 2026, the window that covers all four break-in alarms. It connected to that same stereo 41 times on 19 days in the 30 days before.
